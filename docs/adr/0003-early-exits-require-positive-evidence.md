# Early exits require positive evidence

Leaving the taxi determines when a flight ends. An early-exit marker distinguishes an interruption from a normal landing. Requiring evidence of an interrupting action protects legitimate duration measurements and eligible landing notifications.

Accepting a battlefield port is such an action. Declining the invitation leaves the flight eligible for normal landing behavior. The proposal or invitation alone provides no evidence that travel was interrupted.

Forever's Dungeon Finder does not teleport players. A completed proposal therefore provides no evidence that a taxi flight was interrupted.
